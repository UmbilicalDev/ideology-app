## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an objective, clear, and trustworthy digital experience tailored for political alignment analysis, civic discovery, and ballot comprehension. The brand personality is strictly non-partisan, analytical, and transparent—avoiding polarizing red-and-blue tropes by balancing an electric sky cyan with an impartial solar amber. 

The aesthetic is anchored in **Modern Functionalism with Material Design 3 (M3) ergonomics**:
- **Clarity over ornamentation:** Crisp visual separation and strict contrast ratios ensure users can evaluate sensitive policy stances without visual bias.
- **Tonal integrity:** Deep atmospheric surfaces, pristine content cards, and distinct focal accents guide the eye systematically through questionnaires, ideology matrices, and analytical graphs.
- **Accessibility as a baseline:** Engineered to conform with WCAG AA and AAA standards across both daylight and low-light voter engagement scenarios.

## Colors

The color palette translates the source icon's vivid electric cyan (`#33c6fc`) and radiant gold (`#f0c442`) into an accessible, institutional Material Design 3 system. Raw high-luminance primaries are shifted into mathematically verified tonal ranges to fulfill high-legibility criteria for voting ballots and policy quizzes.

### Application Rules
- **Primary Roles (`Primary` / `Primary Container`):** Designates structural calls-to-action, active survey selections, progress meters, and dominant orientation axes.
- **Secondary Roles (`Secondary` / `Secondary Container`):** Reserved for neutral comparison markers, ideological compass highlights, and bookmarking/save actions.
- **Tertiary Roles (`Tertiary` / `Tertiary Container`):** Used for non-partisan factual indicators, contextual tooltips, and informational tags.
- **Surface Elevation Hierarchy:** Layout depth relies on tonal containers (`Surface Container Lowest` through `Highest`) rather than heavy drop shadows, producing crisp reading layers in both themes.
- **Data Visualization Safety:** When mapping multidimensional political axes (e.g., economic vs. social), use the primary cyan alongside the secondary gold to avoid traditional partisan red/blue bias.

## Typography

The design system uses **Public Sans** across all text hierarchies. As an open-source, government-grade grotesque face, it yields clean legibility, uniform optical balance, and institutional authority—ideal for complex policy queries, constitutional amendments, and candidate manifests.

### Typographic Rhythm
- **Question Stems & Propositions:** Set using `headline-sm` or `title-lg` with a maximum reading line length of 65 characters to avoid scanning fatigue.
- **Explanatory Disclaimers & Methodology:** Rendered in `body-sm` using `on-surface-variant` color token for quiet contextual support.
- **Data Points & Axis Values:** Set in `label-sm` or `label-md` with tabular numerals enabled to maintain baseline alignment across interactive comparative charts.

## Layout & Spacing

Layout geometry follows an 8dp structural system with a 4dp micro-grid for fine adjustments. Content adheres to a fluid responsive grid:
- **Compact (0–599dp):** 4-column layout, 16px (`1rem`) outer margins, 16px gutters. Single-column question cards dominate to keep attention on one topic at a time.
- **Medium (600–839dp):** 8-column layout, 32px (`2rem`) margins, 24px (`1.5rem`) gutters. Side-by-side split layouts accommodate simultaneous reading of policy text and party stances.
- **Expanded (840dp+):** 12-column layout capped at a max-width of 1200px, 48px (`3rem`) margins, 32px (`2rem`) gutters. Designed for spatial visualizations such as compass charts and multidimensional alignment maps.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy conforms to the Material 3 tonal elevation philosophy, avoiding harsh directional shadows in favor of ambient surface shifts.

### Depth Mechanics
- **Level 0 (Flat / Canvas):** `surface` background color.
- **Level 1 (Base Cards / Inactive Items):** `surface-container-low` with a 1px `outline-variant` boundary. No box shadow.
- **Level 2 (Active Question Cards / Selected Options):** `surface-container` paired with a soft ambient shadow (`0 2px 8px -2px rgba(0, 31, 42, 0.08)` in light mode, `0 2px 8px -2px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.4)` in dark mode).
- **Level 3 (Sticky Action Bars / Floating Navigation):** `surface-container-high` accompanied by an ambient blur backdrop filter (`backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`).
- **Level 4 (Dialogs / Candidate Stance Drawers):** `surface-container-highest` with `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(0, 31, 42, 0.16)`.

## Shapes

The shape system adopts a balanced rounded curvature (`roundedness: 2`), reflecting the smooth "squircle" contour present in the brand emblem while maintaining structure for analytical data tables and policy choice cards.

- **Standard Cards & Input Containers:** `rounded-lg` (1rem / 16px).
- **Interactive Buttons & Filter Chips:** Full-pill execution (`rounded-full` / 9999px) to signal tap affordance.
- **Questionnaires & Voting Option Modules:** `rounded` (0.5rem / 8px) inner choice segments nested inside `rounded-xl` (1.5rem / 24px) parent containers.

## Components

### Buttons & Selection Controls
- **Filled Primary Button:** Background `primary`, label `on-primary`. Uses 48px height minimum to ensure accessibility during quick ballot surveys. Hover states use a 8% `on-primary` tint overlay.
- **Tonal Button:** Background `secondary-container`, label `on-secondary-container`. Used for neutral auxiliary actions such as "Skip Question" or "Explain Context".
- **Outlined Button:** Border 1px solid `outline`, text `primary`. Used for tertiary and secondary-tier navigations.

### Questionnaire Likert Scales & Radios
- **Scale Radios:** 5-to-7 point segmented bars spanning "Strongly Disagree" through "Neutral" to "Strongly Agree".
  - Neutral state uses `surface-container-high` and `on-surface-variant`.
  - Agree states shift smoothly into `primary-container` with `primary` outlines.
  - Disagree states resolve into a subtle neutral slate tone (`outline-variant`) rather than alarming red to preserve emotional impartiality.

### Policy & Ideology Cards
- **Card Framing:** Surfaces set to `surface-container-lowest` in light mode and `surface-container-low` in dark mode. Outer frame holds a 1px border of `outline-variant`.
- **Match Indicator Chip:** Pill shape (`rounded-full`), padding `4px 12px`, background `primary-container`, text `on-primary-container`, typography `label-sm`.

### Political Compass & Visualizations
- **Grid Lines:** 1px solid `outline-variant`.
- **User Node:** Filled circle with `brand-cyan-key` (`#33c6fc`), surrounded by a 3px halo of `surface`.
- **Party Alignment Nodes:** Filled circles using `brand-gold-key` (`#f0c442`) and designated secondary values, preventing traditional red/blue partisan identification.
